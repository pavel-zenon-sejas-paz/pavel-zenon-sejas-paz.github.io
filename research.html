Research

My research is on the mathematics of flow in porous media: building models, solving them exactly where possible, and understanding when displacement fronts become unstable. I also work on the hydrodynamic stability of magnetic fluids.

Research interests

Foam flow in porous media

Analytical solutions for foam displacement, including surfactant adsorption, nanoparticle-stabilized foam and layered media; optimal foam slug design; numerical stability of foam simulators.

Hyperbolic conservation laws

Riemann problems for non-strictly hyperbolic systems, admissibility criteria, travelling waves and wave-curve methods applied to multiphase flow.

Displacement instabilities

Saffman–Taylor instability and the competition of viscous fingers, with reduced conservation-law models for finger front velocities.

Underground hydrogen storage

Hydrogen storage in saline aquifers and depleted reservoirs: viscous fingering, gravity segregation, cushion gas and mobility control by foam during injection and withdrawal cycles.

Hydrodynamic stability of magnetic fluids

Stability of flows of ferrofluids under external magnetic fields: linear stability of plane-parallel flows (Orr–Sommerfeld analysis with equilibrium magnetization) and the Plateau–Rayleigh instability of ferrofluid threads through long-wave approximations; perturbation methods. Started in my PhD at UnB and my research stay at UBC.
