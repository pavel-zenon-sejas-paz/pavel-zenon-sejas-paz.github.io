About

I am an applied mathematician working on the mathematical and computational modelling of multiphase flow in porous media. My work combines hyperbolic conservation laws, Riemann problems, stability analysis and numerical methods, with applications to enhanced oil recovery and underground gas storage.

I received my PhD in Mathematics from the University of Brasília in 2020, including a research stay at the University of British Columbia with Neil J. Balmforth. Since 2021 I have worked at LAMAP/UFJF, first as a postdoctoral researcher and, since 2022, as a researcher in an R&D project with Shell Brasil. I am also a team member of research projects funded by CNPq and FAPEMIG.
